## Scope choices and checks

- The one-internal-variable paragraph uses the positive-alphabet port in `SmallEmbedding/ThreePresentation.lean`, rather than pretending the original `Endpoint.Data` structure permits `q=1`. The paper repeats the same construction and uses only `P_C≤u` and the three rows; no stronger one-letter presentation estimate is claimed.
- Physical width is `V=du`, normalized width is `u`; the source `m,d,a,u` and original field are preserved. The endpoint hypothesis needs `u<m`, not `du<m`; the narrow application separately has `du≤m−2`.
- The paper defines competitor ideals using every positive `j`, then derives a finite generating list from the height bound and the least second coordinate at each first coordinate. This is the needed finite stopping argument. The exact periodic bound in [`competitorFiber_stopping`](https://github.com/stksk310/NS-P14-Lean/blob/a5e122a4d09536b1609c5faa8d419f8faf81671f/NSP14/Endpoint/Stopping.lean#L86) was checked but is omitted: Run20 identifies it as historical outside Main. No claim that the final comparison needs this stronger result is made.
- The actual kernel is `M_s∩T_s`. The injection into `E/T_s` controls its socle; finite length of `E/T_s` or of `G` is never asserted.
- `P_C` and `P_G` come from distinct actual quotients. The actual kernel has dimension `P_C−P_G`, and its long exact Tor sequence is used before this difference is cancelled. No quotient monotonicity of Betti numbers is assumed.
- The endpoint homology balance is presented via the actual `v`-cone on the `x`-Koszul complex, matching `HomologyBalance.lean`; no Euler characteristic of infinite-dimensional chain groups is taken.
- The harmonic first-row estimate is stated as the cyclic specialization of the general harmonic Koszul bound, matching `FirstHarmonic.lean` and Run16. The factors `1/(n+1)` and signed differences are numerical rational quantities, never inverses in the coefficient field.
- The positive-row minors are diagonal with entries `±1` over the original field, including characteristic two. No rank identification between different characteristics is invoked.
- The internal quotient is asserted monomial only for `q=2`. The profile `(1,σ_C+1,σ_C)` follows from the actual internal socle and the finite two-variable Euler identity. `σ_C` is distinct from the endpoint socle dimension `R`.
- `W,H_x,P_C,widehat R` are kept in one fixed orientation. The reflected core is not used to bound the original endpoint socle.
