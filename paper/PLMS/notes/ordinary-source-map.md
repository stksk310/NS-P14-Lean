## Checks and scope

An independent subagent recalculated the five small-rank table rows and the large-rank identities using exact symbolic/rational arithmetic. The common gap-shift identity, rank-three polynomial, normalized j=2 weight difference, OL4–OL8, and the two exact seeds all agreed with V06 and the fetched Lean definitions. This is an additional arithmetic check, not a replacement for the Lean proof or a finite-rank extrapolation.

No mathematical bridge remains unresolved in this section. The paper uses only same-jump stratum isomorphisms, never a global module isomorphism T≅N; one fixed cut supplies every resource inequality; relative top j=r is retained; H0 is evaluated exactly in the full transfer; all field extensions preserve the original characteristic and descend through actual source Betti base change. In particular no equality of ranks across different characteristics is asserted. The wide three-generator case is included because the ordinary closure assumes e≥3, not e≥4.

The published-input bibliographic keys are `GreenRestriction`, `APFormula`, and `APCone`, as supplied by the root draft. The original published papers were not independently re-proved or re-refereed during this drafting task; the versions and hypotheses used are those registered in Chapter 1 and connected to actual consumers in the final Run20 source.
